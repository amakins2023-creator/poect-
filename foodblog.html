Building a Healthy Plate

Why a Balanced Meal Matters

A healthy plate gives our body the energy it needs to do our daily activities. We shouldn't forget to drink water, as it helps our kidneys break down food with ease. We should have an intake of at least 3L a day, but if you really want to support your kidneys, the highest amount you can take is 4L per day.
